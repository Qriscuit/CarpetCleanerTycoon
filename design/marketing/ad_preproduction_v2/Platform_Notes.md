# Carpet Cleaner ad platform notes

Retrieved: **29 September 2026**. Primary platform sources only. This is a production reference, not a guarantee of ad approval. The exact campaign objective, placement, market and upload preview still govern delivery.

## Our production choices

- **1080 × 1920, 9:16** master; **15-second** main cut and **25-second** expanded cut. These are our creative choices, not platform-prescribed ideal lengths.
- Produce a broadly compatible MP4 master with H.264 video and AAC audio. Treat these encoding choices as our export settings; the Meta guide's exact current encoding restrictions were not accessible in this research pass.
- Start with the game's existing original music and brush audio. Make brushing audible and preserve a clear picture of the game when muted.
- User confirms Google Play availability. The **actual store URL is still pending**. Do not invent a URL or package name; verify the final listing, app name and destination before attaching the install CTA.

## TikTok: verified published specifications and policy

The June 2026 **Auction In-Feed Ads** specifications distinguish Spark from Non-Spark. For vertical **Non-Spark** video, they list a minimum of **540 × 960**, recommend **9:16**, accept MP4/MOV/MPEG/3GP/AVI, cap files at **500 MB**, and specify bitrate at least **516 kbps**. The page lists a maximum duration of **10 minutes**. Do not generalize these specifications to every TikTok format. [Official auction in-feed specifications](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en)

There is a current documentation discrepancy: TikTok's April 2026 **Ad Format and Functionality** policy states **5–60 seconds**. The same policy requires clear audio, readable high-resolution video, and dynamic content; still images must not occupy more than half of the ad. The proposed 15- and 25-second gameplay edits fit both duration statements. Confirm the selected campaign's upload validation rather than depending on the longer maximum. [Official format and editorial policy](https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-ad-format-and-functionality)

That policy also requires the ad's product, app identity, message and CTA to agree with the destination. Use the real game footage and the correct Google Play listing. [Official consistency requirements](https://ads.us.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-ad-format-and-functionality)

TikTok **recommendations**, rather than upload requirements: vertical 9:16, at least 720p production, sound, readable content inside the UI safe zone, a proposition within the first three seconds, and a hook prioritized within six seconds. These are starting points for testing, not promised performance. [Official creative best practices, June 2025](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)

## Reels: verified guidance and access limit

Meta's official Reels page recommends **9:16 video, audio and important creative elements inside the safe zone**, and points to its safe-zone checker. This supports our vertical master and sound-led cleaning demonstration. It does not establish a conversion forecast for Carpet Cleaner. Consequently, this document **does not claim a verified current Meta maximum duration, file-size limit, minimum resolution or numeric safe-zone margin**. Check those fields in the actual Ads Manager placement before publishing; do not copy them from third-party spec summaries.

## Adaptable framing guides

Our internal conservative guide for the 1080 × 1920 master is **x = 96–900, y = 300–1220** for essential text, game name, focal reveal detail and the visual CTA. This is **an artistic working guide, not an official or universal platform safe zone**. Background and nonessential gameplay can extend across the full frame. Reframe individual shots so the brush action and recognizable pattern remain easy to see.

TikTok explicitly says its safe area varies with video shape, caption length and additional formats. It supplies different templates for standard versus anchor ads and LTR versus Arabic RTL regions; it also warns that previews can differ from live device display. [TikTok safe-zone guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en)

Before publishing, apply the current relevant platform overlay, add the real caption and CTA, and inspect each selected placement's preview. Check the top controls, right interaction rail and bottom caption/CTA area. Move essential elements where needed; the internal rectangle alone does not certify visibility.

## Music and sound

TikTok's official music guidance recommends its **Commercial Music Library (CML)** for promotional content. It says the platform's licenses for music outside that library do not cover commercial use; using original or other external audio with content disclosure requires the Music Usage Confirmation. This was available through the official support page's indexed text; its direct fetch failed. [TikTok commercial music guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok?lang=en)

If using CML instead of our original soundtrack, select the campaign region and filter for the appropriate usable placement. TikTok says selected Commercial Sounds may be used for organic or paid content. [CML instructions, July 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library)

Instagram's official help text says its regular licensed music library is intended for personal, noncommercial use, while **Meta Sound Collection** permits commercial uses such as ads. The official indexed text was available; direct opening was login-blocked. [Instagram music-library guidance](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/402084904469945)

Production decision: use the existing original game music and brush effects for the shared master; retain the source files and provenance notes. Do not assume a track offered in one platform's library is cleared for the other. If music is replaced, check that specific track's platform, placement and market permissions. Mix for intelligible brush detail without clipping, then audition the exported file on a phone speaker and with the sound muted. No universal platform loudness target was verified in this research pass.
