# Soothing carpet previews

Four visual directions generated on 2026-10-01 with the built-in Imagegen tool. All four were integrated into the game on 2026-10-05. These files preserve the original source previews; their unchanged gameplay copies are registered under `CarpetToy/assets/carpet/patterns/` as `stillwater_pond.png`, `misty_tea_garden.png`, `quiet_cove.png`, and `moonlit_greenhouse.png`.

Each PNG is a flat portrait print used as a carpet texture. The visual target is moderate detail, grouped focal points, soft natural colors, and open areas between the forms.
