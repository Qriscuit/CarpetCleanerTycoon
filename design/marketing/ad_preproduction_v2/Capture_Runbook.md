# Capture runbook — discovery and restoration

29 September 2026 · Preproduction only. No footage recorded by this pass.

The 15-second film sells the pleasure of uncovering a rug and appreciating the finished result. Its hero is Fox's Den. Capture enough genuine play to edit **discovery → repeated brushing → accepted full restoration → clean fox → other patterns**. Fifteen seconds is the advertisement's length, not a claim about how quickly a player can clean a rug.

The older [storyboard](../Trailer_Storyboard_v1.md) remains historical: its two-pass starter, pale dirt, missing audio and missing paid-pattern variety are superseded. Current paid rugs use ten passes at a spot, dark soil strength 0.94, and the same starter brush. Paid jobs rotate **Mint Meadow → Sunburst → Fox's Den → Mint Meadow**. All three use the same rug geometry. This brief does not change the game to make the footage faster.

[Creative_Brief.md](Creative_Brief.md) and [shot_manifest.json](shot_manifest.json) are the canonical edit plan. User-confirmed Google Play availability supports the end-card copy **“Carpet Cleaner / Play now / On Google Play.”** The exact listing URL, listing identity and public-build match remain to be verified when the supplied link arrives.

Take C supplies a continuous finish and 2.3 seconds of the real 2.4-second reveal. ### Stroke coordinates for repeatable preparation

Use **rug-local normalized coordinates**, not hard-coded desktop mouse positions: `u=0` is the left bound, `u=1` the right; `v=0` is the far/top fringe and `v=1` the near/bottom fringe in the production overhead view. Current `RUG_HALF = (1.0, 1.66)` gives `x = 2u - 1`, `z = 3.32v - 1.66`; surface height is `y = 0.067`. Resolve local points through the rug transform, then the actual camera, rather than assuming the same screen pixels after resizing.

The inspected Fox reference puts the face around `u=0.20–0.65, v=0.25–0.60`. A useful first rehearsal is a lane near `u=0.35` from `v=0.25` toward `v=0.65`, with a second lane near `u=0.60` if required. These are composition starting points, not a promise that a particular numbered stroke will expose an eye. Select the strongest real mask state during rehearsal. For strokes sweeping dirt past an edge, extend beyond `v=0` or `v=1` while keeping the tool and ejection in view. Check the actual transformed footprint and camera first.

The brush is broad horizontally and moves along the long axis. The starter footprint is approximately 0.61 world units wide; the first wider tool is approximately 0.8784. Do not stretch footage horizontally to improve the comparison.

## Camera, composition and HUD

- Produce a **1080 × 1920, 9:16** editing master as a production choice. Rehearse the actual game at a 9:16 viewport first. Existing discovery screenshots are 390 × 844, and the old storyboard references are 720 × 1280; neither set proves the final crop. Do not stretch either aspect ratio.
- Production paid cleaning currently uses an overhead camera; its layout reserves room for progress and earnings controls. The completion reveal has its own closer, unobstructed framing. Use these implemented views for the primary footage. Gym's optional oblique camera is a separate practice view, not an advertised paid camera control.
- Keep the entire brush head, its point of contact and the changing soil boundary together during a continuous stroke. A restrained crop around the face may exclude some outer rug and HUD. It must not exclude the crucial contact, hide a mismatched tool or turn a normal lane into a misleading whole-rug wipe.
- Remove HUD through an editorial crop only where it remains visually comfortable. Retain enough of a completion shot to establish the real finish if used as a gameplay beat. A separate full-rug postcard can omit the completion heading. No dedicated clean-capture HUD toggle currently exists; hiding nodes is additional capture tooling, not a setting already available to players.
- Use the pack's common provisional working region **x96–900 / y300–1220 on the 1080 × 1920 canvas** for essential text, the face and brush contact. This is an internal composition guide, **not an official universal safe zone**. Surrounding rug and floor may fill the frame. Final text, CTA, face, brush head and cleanup edge must be checked against the actual Reels/TikTok placement preview, caption and buttons. Adjust each placement's crop after that check.
- Do not insert a drawn finger, exaggerated swipe trail or fake interactive controls into the captured game. If an editorial cue is needed, keep it visually separate from gameplay and avoid making it look tappable.
- Review at approximately phone display size with sound off. If the face does not read, change the recorded lane/crop or opening checkpoint; adding more copy is not the first remedy.

`--shop-test` deliberately mutes physical audio unless **`--audition-audio`** is also passed after `--`. Both flags are implemented in `game_audio.gd`. Capture synchronized game effects with the live performance. Prefer a separate editorial music bed from the existing music asset so cuts between takes do not jump around its phrase; avoid doubling the in-game music and the editorial bed. The Home Music/Sound switches allow a music-off/effects-on take. Test-save audio preferences are process-specific and separate from the user's preferences.

There is no verified production coin-tick, purchase-click, hose or squeegee sound system in the current four-voice audio controller. Do not list those effects as available recordings. Additional editorial sounds would need an explicit asset/licensing choice and a listening pass. The minimal film can work with existing brush, completion and music. Sound is support; muted comprehension remains necessary.

## Capture workflow for the next production turn

This document does not launch the engine, record a window, create new save fixtures or write raw footage. The next capture turn should do the following in order.

1. **Freeze source facts.** Record the current source revision plus any working-tree changes, engine version, rug definitions, brush tier and relevant audio state in the take manifest. Confirm the 99% full-only polish is included. Preserve both the uncut native-speed gameplay and the edit decisions.
2. **Isolate all temporary state under the workspace.** Use a new session directory, for example `build/ad-capture-v2/session-001/`, containing `profile/AppData/Roaming/`, `profile/AppData/Local/`, `saves/`, `checkpoints/` and `logs/`. Set `APPDATA` and `LOCALAPPDATA` only in the capture process environment; do not repurpose system variables or modify a normal player profile.
3. **Require the real test mode.** Always launch through `tools/run_godot.ps1` with `-- --shop-test`; use the full `powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File` form so the argument separator is forwarded. Add `--audition-audio` when recording sound. The wrapper checks the complete **Godot 4.7.2 stable Mono/.NET** bundle. No engine from PATH and no older/standard binary is acceptable.
4. **Use production gameplay, not the validation movie.** The currently verified interactive launch shape is `powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ./tools/run_godot.ps1 --path CarpetToy -- --shop-test --audition-audio`. This command is documented for later use; it was not executed by this pass. The test ledger normally writes `CarpetToy/.godot/shop_test_<pid>.json` and audio settings `CarpetToy/.godot/audio_test_<pid>.cfg`. Archive those exact generated test files under the session's `saves/`; never copy from, reset or overwrite `user://neighborhood_shop_v1.json` in a real player profile.
5. **Choose and prove the recording route before full takes.** A real-time window recording of the actual game is the preferred source because it preserves real input timing and live audio. A recorder has not been verified in this audit. Check what is available in the next turn and record a short sync/quality sample. If using Godot's movie writer instead, first verify the pinned engine's supported command flags and output/audio behavior; this runbook deliberately does not invent an untested movie command. Run one renderer at a time and keep focus on the game, because focus loss stops effects.
6. **Set a real 9:16 render view.** The repository already demonstrates `root.size = Vector2i(...)` in capture/validation scripts. A small isolated capture harness can use the same API for 1080 × 1920 if window recording/resizing cannot provide it cleanly. That harness would be new work in the next capture turn. It must require `--shop-test`, use the production scene, retain arrival/completion animations, and leave scene shaders, brush strength and reward rules unchanged. Do not change checked-in project defaults just to force the shot.
7. **Prepare reproducible, honest checkpoints.** Ideally play the first two paid rugs and keep their clean reveals, then save a partly cleaned Fox at suitable hook and finishing stages. Copy only the isolated test ledger while the capture process is stopped, or explicitly redirect a future test-only harness's `_save_path` into `saves/`. Record rug ID, job ID, mask origin and preparation method. Restoring checkpoints is for additional takes, not a claim of one continuous session. A resumed comparison must retain “Before this session.”
8. **Record source takes at ordinary timing.** If later capture tooling drives input, it must use the same production input path with frame-by-frame elapsed time and normal contact behavior. It must not loop dozens of strokes in one rendered frame or call multiple physics updates between recorded frames. Record the terminal stroke through its real threshold, suction and reveal without setting masks or granting a reward during the take.
9. **Stage artifacts separately.** Put uncut video/audio into `design/marketing/ad_preproduction_v2/raw/session-001/`, selected stills into `.../stills/session-001/`, the shot log into `.../takes/session-001.json`, and review/export cuts into `.../edits/` and `.../exports/`. These are proposed output locations, not assets already generated. Keep raw sources immutable; version edits instead of overwriting the only take.
10. **Check before editing.** Confirm actual video dimensions/frame rate, native playback speed, stable frames, audio sync, no desktop notifications/cursor obstruction, correct rug identity, matching brush tier and the fully polished real result. Desktop capture proves presentation on that machine; it does not establish Android frame time or touch latency. If a phone is available later, repeat the key feel/performance check there.

### Why the existing fixture tools are not finished footage

- `design/marketing/capture_storyboard.gd` makes staged reference PNGs. It seeds currency/unlocked stores, skips arrival animations, uses tight brush/physics loops without wall-clock waits, and clears masks for its clean reference. Its own manifest explains these conditions. Running a recorder on that script would not produce an honest player-speed demonstration.
- `tools/validate_rug_discovery.gd` writes actual renderer screenshots, but deliberately installs result masks, advances physics manually and stages comparison/continuity cases. Its clean/partial stills are useful for framing and art review, not proof of an earned completion or job duration.
- `tools/validate_completion_reveal.gd` tests real reveal behavior using completion fixtures. Use it to check the product, not as an unmarked substitute for the final gameplay take.
- Existing images under `art/renders/discovery_*.png` and `design/marketing/references/` remain **fixture stills**. Do not rename or label them as captured ad footage. If a review board uses them, keep the fixture label in its caption/manifest.

## Minimal gaps to resolve during capture, not a new feature backlog

The game already has the key visual story: dark soil, immediate dirt response, recognizable fox, full-only final polish, completion hold and three patterns. The remaining practical work is a verified video/audio recording path, final 9:16 framing, an honest repeatable late-pass hook, and a small uncut capture review. If the ten-pass opening does not visibly reward curiosity within its chosen excerpt, change the excerpt or make the limitation explicit; do not secretly strengthen the brush. If the short clean hold is insufficient for the title, use a documented editorial frame hold on the end card rather than changing completion behavior just for the ad.

Use the confirmed end-card plan **“Carpet Cleaner / Play now / On Google Play.”** Google Play availability is confirmed by the user; the exact listing URL, public listing name/icon and agreement between the public build and shown features remain pending verification. Do not replace this copy with a generic CTA placeholder, invent a store badge or add ratings/download counts. No ad publishing or paid campaign is part of this runbook.
