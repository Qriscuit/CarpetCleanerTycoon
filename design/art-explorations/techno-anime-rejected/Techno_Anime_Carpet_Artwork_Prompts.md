Generated on 2026-10-01 with the built-in Imagegen tool. Six new designs target a medium level of detail: strong central subjects, a few grouped supporting elements, and open areas between them. Godot imports use mipmaps and the existing 1024-pixel size limit.

## neon_panther

Asset: `CarpetToy/assets/carpet/patterns/neon_panther.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ONE production game carpet albedo texture, portrait 1024x1536, 2:3. The ENTIRE canvas is the flat rectangular print, no physical rug, fringe, room, perspective view of rug, shadows, watermarks or text. Art direction: a carefully composed contemporary techno/anime illustration with MEDIUM detail density. Use 65-75% of the picture for a strong central subject plus two or three grouped supporting elements, with 25-35% restful flat background distributed around those groups. Visually active in upper, middle and lower areas, not all at bottom. Big readable shapes first, selective smaller details second. Clear hierarchy and intentional asymmetry where suitable. Clean confident linework and cel-shaded or graphic flat colors, 6-8 coordinated colors. Some detail, not minimal emptiness and not dense allover ornamental pattern. No filigree, random tiny filler, repeated symbol wallpaper, ultra-fine linework, words, numbers, pseudo-lettering, logos or grunge. Subtle matte woven surface at most; clean textile print colors, not aged paper. Border can use simple purposeful angular corner accents within outer 5% rather than an elaborate frame. Critical motif kept 8% inside edges. Designed to look beautiful and recognizable when seen as a 200-pixel-wide rug.
Theme: Neon Panther. A large powerful elegant black-and-indigo panther in a flowing C-shaped crouch across the center, face three-quarter view facing viewer with two bright cyan eyes and magenta edge accents, one long tail curving upward. Coherent feline anatomy with a readable face and paws. Behind its shoulders is one offset large coral-magenta circle, and behind the tail a broad pale-cyan angular arch. Two small clusters of 3 chunky circuit traces along opposing corners, and three broad dynamic diagonal streaks in the lower corner. Bright silver-blue inner field with deep navy silhouette, cyan, magenta, coral and violet blocks. Strong stylish techno poster composition, not a repeated circuit pattern. Flat printed ink, not photoreal neon glow. Occupy the full canvas with this grouped composition but keep clear spaces between supporting geometry and animal.
